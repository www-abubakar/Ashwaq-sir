INSTITUTE POLICY

Terms & Conditions

Students and parents are expected to follow these rules.

01. Discipline & Respect

All students must maintain respectful behaviour towards teachers, staff and fellow students.

02. Student Behaviour

Disturbing classes, inappropriate behaviour or activities affecting the learning environment are prohibited.

03. Attendance & Timings

Students must attend classes regularly and arrive on time. Students must follow institute entry and departure timings communicated by the institute.

04. Fighting & Bullying

Fighting, bullying, threatening and creating disputes among students are prohibited.

05. Respect for Teachers

Students must respect teachers, staff and fellow students at all times.

06. Leaving the Institute

Students may not leave institute premises during class hours without permission.

07. Prohibited Items

Drugs, tobacco, alcohol, weapons and other prohibited or harmful items are not allowed on institute premises.

08. Fee Policy

Fees must be paid regularly according to the institute's payment schedule. Holiday and vacation fee policies will be communicated to parents at admission.

09. Mobile Phones

Students may not use mobile phones during classes without permission.

10. Fee Due Date

Fees must be paid within the specified due date. Late payment may result in temporary suspension of classes after prior communication with parents.

11. Parent Cooperation

Parents or guardians are expected to cooperate regarding attendance, discipline, academic progress and fee payments.

12. Institute Authority

The institute may take appropriate disciplinary action for repeated violations after communicating with the parent or guardian.

Declaration: By enrolling their child at Ashwaq Institute, parents/guardians and students acknowledge that they have read and understood the institute's rules and agree to follow them.
